# Sprint 12 Pilot Calibration Protocol v1

**Status:** `G2_APPROVED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`

## Qualification

Each annotator must demonstrate Vietnamese/English/Japanese competence for the
slices they label, understand BrSE project semantics, complete the annotation
guide calibration, disclose conflicts and sign the no-data-copy and
independence rules. A language slice without a qualified annotator is removed
or reduced explicitly before G2.

## Calibration procedure

1. The annotation lead distributes common examples from a calibration-only set.
2. Annotators label the common examples independently and discuss only the
   written guide rules, not pilot labels.
3. The lead records questions and guide clarifications before pilot labeling.
4. The 20 pilot cases and 3 episodes are then assigned with isolated inputs and
   no shared intermediate labels.
5. A third qualified reviewer adjudicates material disagreements.

The agent-created labels in this directory exercise the protocol and evaluator
only. They do not satisfy the human qualification or independence evidence for
G2.
